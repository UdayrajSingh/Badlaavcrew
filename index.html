What is Badlaav Crew?

Badlaav means change. We’re three students starting from scratch with one belief: change doesn’t have to begin big — it has to begin somewhere. Through creative projects, handmade products and fundraising efforts, we aim to support meaningful causes and help create more opportunities for children, including deaf and blind children.
